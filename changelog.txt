Version: 0.4.1
Date: 02. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - Added Fuel Stats: change the energy value, vehicle acceleration and top speed of every vanilla and Space Age fuel. Replaces the standalone mod Adjustable Fuel Stats.
    - Added Recycler Return Rate: set how much the recycler gives back, from 1 to 100 % (vanilla 25 %), for items that recycle into themselves only (default) or every recycling recipe except scrap, to tune quality upcycling loops. Replaces the standalone mod Adjustable Recycler.
  Changes:
    - Cheat settings have their own entry in the Settings Hub list.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fuel Stats, fixed compared to Adjustable Fuel Stats: the default values match the game, so untouched settings no longer change any fuel, and Yumako, Jellynut, Yumako mash, Jelly, Bioflux and Nutrients can be changed too.
    - Recycler Return Rate, fixed compared to Adjustable Recycler: the rate is applied once, so 25 % really means vanilla, and it works on Factorio 2.1.
  Info:
    - Requires Exteros' QoL System 0.3.4 (Factorio 2.0) or 0.4.4 (Factorio 2.1).
    - The mod download is smaller: the thumbnail was compressed.
