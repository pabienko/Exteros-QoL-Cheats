Version: 1.0.0
Date: 14. 11. 2026
  Major Features:
    - Rebuilt on Factorio 2.1 only; the 2.0 line ends here.
    - Standalone mod now: depends only on Exteros' Lib, not on Exteros' QoL.
    - Cheat Mode is a runtime switch, changed through Exteros' Hub (SHIFT + E).
  Changes:
    - Productivity, Stack Size, and Roboport Range stay plain startup settings with no master switch.
  Bugfixes:
    - Internal fixes and improvements.
  Upgrading from 0.4.x:
    - Cheat Mode is off after the update and its bonuses are removed; turn it back on in Exteros' Hub.
    - No longer needs Exteros' QoL, only Exteros' Lib. Older versions can't load next to Exteros' QoL 1.0.0.

Date: 02. 10. 2026

  Bugfixes:
